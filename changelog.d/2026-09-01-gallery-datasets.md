The component gallery now serializes deterministic synthetic `UserProfile` datasets into its temporary directory and exposes rich, single-run, short-history, and bunched-history states through an in-gallery selector.
Synthetic runs use representative 30–90 second durations and derive accuracy, kills, damage, and score from coherent shot outcomes; multi-hour history states are assembled from ordinary runs rather than inflated run lengths.
Restored the production dashboard, playtime, and completion-history graph panels against the real view models, and replaced the obsolete expression-tree mockups with the production `ExpressionTreeEditor` and a real editable expression model.

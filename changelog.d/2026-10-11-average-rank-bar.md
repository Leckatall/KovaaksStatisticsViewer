Mapping: `BenchmarkProjection::averageRank` is the mean of every scenario's `normalizedRank` (tiers attained plus the in-tier fraction toward the next; PB / first threshold below it; exactly the tier count above the top), so it is already on the tier bar's chunk scale. New `rankChunkFills(rank, tierCount)` (`benchmark_progress_grid_model.{h,cpp}`) fills chunk k (0-based) with `clamp(rank - k, 0, 1)`: exactly k ends at chunk k's right edge and a fraction fills the next chunk proportionally (3.5 of five tiers: Iron, Bronze, Silver full, Gold half).
`BenchmarkTrackingViewModel` gains `averageRankFills` (empty outside the trackable state or without an average) and `completedRankPosition` (1-based index of the completed tier, the tick's chunk boundary, or -1).
`TierBar.qml` gains `tick`/`tickDescription`/`tickHovered`: a 2px tick overhanging the bar on the boundary after chunk `tick`, with its own tooltip and hover target.
`BenchmarkStatusSummary.qml`: the "Average rank" row shows a TierBar (tiers from the progress grid, 160-320px wide) instead of the decimal; `summaryAverageRank` is now the bar's container, keeps the formatted number as its `text`, accessible name and tooltip ("Average rank 3.5"), and hides that tooltip while the completed-rank tick ("Completed rank: Silver") is hovered. Attained rank and next tier stay as text in their tier colours.
Tests: `RankChunkFills.WholeRanksEndAtTheirChunksRightEdgeAndFractionsFillTheNext`, `BenchmarkTrackingViewModel.AverageRankBarFillsAndCompletedRankTick` (ui-cpp), `test_summaryAverageRankIsATierBarWithACompletedRankTick`, `test_summaryWithoutACompletedRankHasNoTick` (tst_BenchmarkWorkspace.qml).
Review fix: the summary's `GridLayout` has `uniformCellWidths: true`. The bar row's larger implicit width had made the layout give the left column most of the card, pushing "Completed rank" and "Total playtime" from the card's middle (x≈630 at 1280) to x≈920; both columns are equal halves again. `test_summaryAverageRankIsATierBarWithACompletedRankTick` checks the right-hand fields stay in the card's right half.

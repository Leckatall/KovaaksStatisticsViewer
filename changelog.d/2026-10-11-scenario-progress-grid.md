type: changed
area: Benchmarks
user: The Benchmarks tab now lists every scenario in one grid laid out like the Benchmark Manager's table, with a tier-coloured progress bar per scenario that runs all the way to the top tier. Scenarios that hold back your next rank are marked in that rank's colour. This replaces the separate blocker list and breakdown table.
---
New `BenchmarkProgressGridModel` (`src/ui/presentation/benchmark_progress_grid_model.{h,cpp}`) replaces `BenchmarkBreakdownModel`: a flat, definition-ordered row list (Uncategorized first, then each category's direct scenarios and subcategories), group spans (`groups`: column, id, name, raw colour, start row, span; empty groups left out), the tier ladder (`tiers`: name, readable text colour, opaque bar colour) and `hasSubcategories`. It is owned by `BenchmarkTrackingViewModel` (`progressGrid`, CONSTANT) and reset once per use-case revision from the selected definition joined with its projection; registered in `ksv::declare_metatypes()`.
`scenarioChunkFills(tiers, thresholds, score)` is the bar mapping: chunk k fills `clamp((score - t[k-1]) / (t[k] - t[k-1]), 0, 1)`, chunk 0 from 0; a chunk whose bounding thresholds are missing, duplicated, negative, non-finite or not increasing stays empty. Its filled length in chunk units equals `ScenarioProjection::normalizedRank` whenever the ladder is valid (tested against `domain::normalizedTierValue`).
Per row: PB and Recent in the shared `formatBenchmarkNumber` form ("—" when absent; Recent adds the sample count below five runs), next tier name/colour plus threshold ("Highest tier attained" only when every chunk is full), bar fills, a bar description ("Silver · 50% of the way to Gold (5,670 / 5,954)", "Unranked · …", "Unplayed", "… · top tier reached", "no threshold set for …", "the X threshold is not above Y's") with the abnormal match state and its hint appended, and the blocker flag/colour/text ("Blocks Gold (needs 4,800)") from `nextTierBlockers`.
QML: new `TierBar.qml` (reusable segmented bar: equal-width chunks, tier-coloured fills over a dim track, whole bar is the tooltip/hover target, chunk geometry from `chunkLeft/chunkRight`) and `BenchmarkProgressGrid.qml` (header in the editor's style, tier headers with swatch and 4px tier underline over the exact chunk boundaries, group cells spanning their rows with the 0.22 category tint, diamond blocker marker with tooltip, columns measured over all rows; the bar column takes the remaining width between one minimum chunk per tier and 160px per chunk; when too narrow the scenario column elides down to 120px and the grid then scrolls horizontally in its own Flickable with an always-on scroll bar below it).
`BenchmarkWorkspace.qml`: the grid sits directly under the "N of M scenarios at the next tier" line in both the trackable and incomplete bodies; the history cards follow it.
Removed `BenchmarkBlockerList.qml`, `BenchmarkBreakdownView.qml`, `BenchmarkBreakdownNode.qml`, `BenchmarkBreakdownModel` and its test, and the view model's `blockers` list, `blockerProgress` and `setExpanded` (the grid has no expansion state).
Tests: `tests/ui/benchmark_progress_grid_model_test.cpp` (fraction mapping below the first tier, on a threshold, mid-tier, above the top tier, missing and non-increasing thresholds, parity with the domain normalized rank; row order, group spans, empty groups, tier colours, value formatting, descriptions, match states, blockers, reset), updated `benchmark_tracking_vm_test.cpp`, `benchmark_composition_test.cpp` and `tst_BenchmarkWorkspace.qml` (row order, group spans, header/value alignment, chunk/header boundary alignment, tier-coloured fills, blocker markers, narrow-width scrolling, 150% font). The `full` tour hovers the first blocker marker and tier bar instead of the removed blocker list and breakdown toggle.
Review fix: the first and last tier header cells also cover the bar column's 8px inset (`leadingInset`/`trailingInset`, name kept centred over the chunk with `horizontalCenterOffset`), so the inset no longer shows the plain Progress header cell as two narrow empty columns beside the tier headers.

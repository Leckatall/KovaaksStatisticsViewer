`missingThresholdChunks(tiers, thresholds)` (`benchmark_progress_grid_model.{h,cpp}`) flags each chunk whose own threshold or the previous tier's is missing, duplicated, negative or non-finite, or not increasing (the same chunks `scenarioChunkFills` leaves empty).
Each progress-grid row gains `missingChunks`, `edgeState` ("" | "unplayed" | "unmatched" | "aboveTop"), `statusText` and `aboveTopText`. Unresolved, Ambiguous and AutoMappable match states are "unmatched" with the user-facing match text ("Not found in your runs", "Several scenarios match", "Linking to your runs…") as status; a scenario without a PB is "unplayed" ("Unplayed", or "Unplayed · no runs of the linked scenario" for a MappedUnavailable mapping, which is what an unplayed demo scenario is); a PB strictly above a valid top threshold with every chunk full is "aboveTop" ("Above Platinum: 5,670 / 5,400"). Exactly on the top threshold is reached, not exceeded.
`TierBar.qml` gains `missing`, `edgeState`, `statusText`, `aboveTopText`: missing chunks get clipped diagonal hatching; "unmatched" outlines the whole bar in amber (#FFB74D, the old breakdown's abnormal-status colour) over the base colour with the status in amber inside it; "unplayed" shows a muted italic label inside the empty track; "aboveTop" adds a small diamond in the top tier's colour at the bar's end with its own tooltip. None of these states draws a partial fill. The full hint stays in the bar tooltip.
Tests: `MissingThresholdChunks.FlagsChunksWithAnUnusableBound`, `BenchmarkProgressGridModel.EdgeStatesUnplayedUnmatchedAndAboveTheTopTier`, `BenchmarkProgressGridModel.MissingThresholdChunksTravelWithTheRow` (ui-cpp), `test_tierBarEdgeStatesEachHaveTheirOwnLook` (tst_BenchmarkWorkspace.qml).
Review fix: `missingThresholdChunks` treats a threshold out of order with a neighbour (not above the one before, or not below the one after) as unusable, since either of the pair may be the wrong one, and flags every chunk it bounds; `scenarioChunkFills` zeroes every flagged chunk. Before, Iron 1800 / Bronze 1200 / Silver 2400 hatched only Bronze and filled Silver 66% from the doubtful 1200 bound (the incomplete demo's Smoothbot row). `TierChunkFills.ChunksBoundedByAnOutOfOrderThresholdStayEmpty` replaces `AChunkWhoseThresholdIsNotAboveThePreviousStaysEmpty`; new `MissingThresholdChunks.AnOutOfOrderThresholdHatchesEveryChunkItBounds`.

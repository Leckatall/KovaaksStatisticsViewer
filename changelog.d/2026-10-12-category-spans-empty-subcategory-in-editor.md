`BenchmarkEditorTable.qml` mirrors the progress grid's rule (077774c). The editor is a TableView of per-cell delegates rather than group overlays, so the rule is per row: new `spansSubcategory(columnKind, subcategoryId)` / `coveredBySpan(columnKind, subcategoryId)` helpers say that a row without a subcategory id (scenarios straight under their category, or Uncategorized; the data layer never mixes direct scenarios and subcategories in one category) has a spanning Category cell and a covered Subcategory cell. No model change.
`TableCell` now draws everything inside a child `face` Rectangle (objectName `cellFace`, exposed as `cell.face`); the cell itself is transparent. TableView forces each delegate to its column's width, so a spanning cell's face is Category + Subcategory wide and the cell sits at `z: 1` to draw over its neighbour; pointer handlers live on the face, so a click anywhere in the span selects the Category cell. The covered Subcategory cell gets `opacity: 0`, `enabled: false` and `Accessible.ignored`. The group swatch, add-subcategory "+", subcategory creation field and the edit delegate (`width: cell.face.width`) all use the full span.
Keyboard: Right from a spanning Category cell goes to Scenario and Left from Scenario returns to Category; `redirectCoveredCurrent()` (in the selection model's `onCurrentChanged`) moves any current index that lands on a covered cell (e.g. Up/Down from a real subcategory cell) to that row's Category cell. Group drop targeting (`targetAtScene`) hit-tests the face.
The editor's identifying-column widths are fixed (`identityWidths`), so unlike the grid there is no width measuring to adjust. Covered by `test_categoryWithoutSubcategoriesSpansTheSubcategoryColumn` in `tst_BenchmarkEditorTable.qml`.

`TierBar.qml`: the recent-average marker is now a full constraint in threshold-label placement, not just a tie-breaker. `labelPlacementAt` takes the first of end, middle, start that is clear of both the value (PB) label and the marker; only when none is clear of both does it fall back to the first place clear of the PB (as before, so avoiding the PB still wins and the label may sit under the marker). Previously a label only moved to avoid the PB, and the marker merely ranked the moved places, so a label at its chunk's end stayed under the marker (VT FlyTS Iron "3,500", Pasu "3,350" in both fixtures).
`coversMarker` measures against the tick's drawn half-width (`markerHalfWidth`, 2) plus `markerGap` (2), i.e. the same ±4 px around the line as before for plain text; a label that would be a target at that placement (`labelInPartialFillAt`) is measured with its outline (`targetPad`, 2 px each side, now also used by the `_target_` outline geometry), so the outline box stays clear of the line, not just the digits. The target decision itself is unchanged and still follows the chosen placement.
The PB label never moves, so when the marker crosses it (`markerCrossesValue`) the tick is cut behind the text: `<bar>_markerTick` shrinks to the top overhang plus a couple of px into the bar and `<bar>_markerTickBottom` draws the matching bottom end, both stopping short of the digits (Smoothbot's "1,995" inside the Platinum fill at 960). The marker's hover target and tooltip are unchanged.
Tests in `tst_BenchmarkWorkspace.qml`: a label moving off the marker (including a target whose outline, not text, would touch it), the fallback under the marker when the PB blocks every other place or the chunk is too narrow for any clear place, and the cut tick over the PB.

Reverses the chunk meaning introduced by `cb8fc1a` ("each tier-bar chunk is the range in which that tier is held"), adapted by hand because `a1f50ec` changed the same files since. That commit's fragment (`2026-10-12-tier-bar-chunks-hold-the-rank.md`) is deleted with this change: it was never released, and its `user:` line would describe behaviour that no longer exists.
Chunk k (0-based) is again the progress toward tier k: from t[k-1] (0 for k = 0) to t[k]. The filled length in chunk units equals `domain::normalizedTierValue` whenever the ladder is valid (6.37 = six full chunks), the same scale as the rank graph and the average-rank tooltip.
`scenarioChunkFills` (`benchmark_progress_grid_model.{h,cpp}`) fills chunk k with clamp((score - t[k-1]) / (t[k] - t[k-1]), 0, 1), with the zero-first-threshold special case back. `kEnteredChunkFill` is removed: a chunk is complete exactly when its tier is reached, so no sliver is needed, and `TierBar.fillWidthAt` no longer enforces `minimumFillWidth` (removed) and just rounds chunk width x fill.
`missingThresholdChunks` hatches chunk k when its own threshold or the previous tier's (k > 0) is unusable.
`rankChunkFills` (summary bar) is clamp(rank - k, 0, 1) per chunk again, and `BenchmarkTrackingViewModel::completedRankPosition` is the completed tier's 1-based index, i.e. the chunk-scale position of the end of its chunk. `TierBar.tick` keeps its geometry (position on the chunk scale, 0 = bar start, chunkCount = bar end); only its comment changed.
`tierScalePosition` (recent-average marker) returns the domain's normalized value unchanged, so a recent average below the first tier sits inside Iron's chunk instead of being clamped to the bar's start. `progressDescription`, the row's `recentDescription` tier, "Highest tier attained" and the `aboveTop` test keep the threshold-based logic from `cb8fc1a`, which does not depend on the chunk meaning.
`TierBar` threshold labels are right-aligned at the end of their chunk (inset `labelInset`, 3 px) as the score that completes the chunk, and `coversValue` tests that right-aligned span against the PB value label. The PB value label stays in the attained rank's colour.
Tests: the C++ fill, missing-chunk, marker-scale, rank-fill and completed-rank expectations are back on the progress-toward-tier scale (`FilledLengthMatchesTheDomainNormalizedRankAndTheMarkerScale` checks both); the QML fixtures (`TestDoubles.js`) and `tst_BenchmarkWorkspace` (tick at the end of Silver's chunk, right-aligned labels, PB/marker chunk indices, a PB near its chunk's end hiding that chunk's score) follow.

`BenchmarkProgressGrid.qml`: column order is now Category, Subcategory, Scenario, PB date, Plays, bar, Next tier (`_layoutColumns` place order and the row separator list).
Tier header cells (`progressTierHeader_i`) drop the 14 px swatch; the name label (`progressTierHeader_i_name`) is the cell's only content besides the 4 px tier underline, and is coloured by the new `readableOn(barColor, palette.button, palette.buttonText)`: the bar colour when it reaches WCAG 4.5:1 on the header background, otherwise the first 5% blend toward the header text colour that does. It is computed in QML against the real header palette rather than reusing the model's `color` (which `readableTextColor` tunes against the darker #343434 panel, so e.g. Iron #9b9ba3 and Grandmaster #ee7785 came out at about 4:1 on the #3c3c3c header).
Chunk widths: `_measured.chunk` is now the widest bold tier name plus `chunkPadding` (6 px) on each side, and the new `_measured.chunkMinimum` is that name plus `chunkSlack` (4 px). `_layoutColumns` grows the bar between `chunk` and the 160 px cap as before, but when short of width it first shrinks the chunks from `chunk` to `chunkMinimum`, then elides the scenario names down to `scenarioMinimum`, then scrolls.
With the `rich` driver dataset the 12-tier `driver-many-tiers` fixture still needs 1417 px (grid width at a 1280 window is 1248, so it scrolls by about 170 px; before this change it needed 1753 px). The 5-tier `driver-demo` fixture now fits at 960 without scrolling.
Tests in `tst_BenchmarkWorkspace.qml`: column order, header without swatch with a readable per-tier name colour and kept underline, `readableOn` on dark and light headers, and the padding-before-scenario order of `_layoutColumns`.

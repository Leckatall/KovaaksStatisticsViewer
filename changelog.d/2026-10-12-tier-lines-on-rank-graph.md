user: The Benchmarks "Personal-best average rank" graph draws a line in each tier's colour at every tier level, and the rank line itself is now neutral (the text colour) instead of orange, so it can no longer be mistaken for Gold or Bronze. Every tier gets its own line even when there are too many tiers to label them all.
---
`SeriesModel` gains `referenceLines` (`ReferenceLine{value, color}`, display units on the series' own Y axis). `GraphCanvas::drawReferenceLines()` paints them for every visible series after the series lines and before the marked-x rule, as full-width horizontal lines `kReferenceLineWidth` (2px, gridlines are 1px) thick, so a tier level stays legible where the rank line runs along it (accepted trade-off: the tier line then covers the rank line).
An invalid `SeriesModel::color` now means "neutral": `GraphCanvas` has a new `foregroundColor` property (set to `palette.text` by `GraphCanvasWithTooltip`) and `colorFor()` resolves the line, marked-x ring, `valuesAtX` and `nearestPoint` colours through it, so the tooltip swatch and hover dot match the drawn line. Every other graph sets explicit colours and is unaffected.
`BenchmarkHistoryViewModel::update()` takes `std::vector<RankTier>` (name + colour) instead of tier names; `BenchmarkTrackingViewModel::rebuild()` passes each tier's `readableTextColor` (the same colour the tier names use on the page). For AverageRank the series colour is cleared and one reference line per tier is set at y = 1..tierCount; Unranked (y = 0) gets none.
The rank Y axis now asks for `2 * tierCount` target intervals so the Heckbert step resolves to 1 (12 tiers over 12 intervals rounded up to a step of 2, which is why the 12-tier fixture only had every other tier on the axis); `integral` keeps it at exactly 1. The axis painter still drops labels by stride when they would collide, so on a short card with many tiers only every other tier is labelled, but each has its line. The left margin is measured over all ticks, so it now fits the longest tier name even when that tier's label is skipped.
Tests: `benchmark_history_vm_test.cpp` pins one reference line per tier in its readable colour, the neutral rank series, the playtime series keeping its colour with no lines, a tick at every tier for 3..25 tiers, and the canvas resolving the neutral colour through `foregroundColor` (and `valuesAtX`). `BenchmarkHistoryUiTest.BothHistoryCanvasesPaintTheirSeries` now uses `canvas->colorFor()` and an unreachable tier threshold, since its old fixture put the rank line exactly on the single tier's line.

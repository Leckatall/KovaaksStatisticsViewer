type: fixed
area: Benchmarks
user: In the Benchmark Manager, dragging the library divider all the way toward the editor no longer hides the rank columns: the Category, Subcategory and Scenario columns narrow to fit their names, and the editor stops shrinking while one whole rank column and the rank scrollbar are still in view.
---
Old bug: the editor pane's `SplitView.minimumWidth` was a literal 360, while `BenchmarkEditorTable`'s identifying columns were pinned at `identityWidths` [130, 120, 200] = 450 px (minimum = preferred = maximum in `tableBody`). Squeezed to 360, the RowLayout gave the identity column its 450 and the rank side (the `rankHeader`, `thresholdView` and the `thresholdHorizontalBar` parented into `thresholdBody`) 0 px past the window's right edge, so no rank, no add-rank "+" and no scrollbar showed, and the Scenario column itself was cut off.
`BenchmarkEditorTable.qml`: `identityWidths` is now computed. `identityPreferredWidths` keeps [130, 120, 200]; `identityContentWidths` is, per column, the widest of its header label, its "+ Add ..." control and every row's text (bold group names plus the label's 6px margins and the swatch / add-subcategory clearance from `TableCell`; plain scenario names, "Unnamed scenario" for an empty one), capped at the preferred width. When `tableBody.width` leaves less than the preferred total plus `rankSlotWidth` plus one `rankHeaderMinimumWidth`, each column gives up the same fraction of its slack (preferred minus content), floored, down to its content width. A spanning Category is measured in column 0 alone, which is conservative. `identityView.forceLayout()` runs (via `Qt.callLater`) when the widths change, since its `columnWidthProvider` is not a binding.
New `minimumTableWidth` = content widths + `rankSlotWidth` + `rankHeaderMinimumWidth`; `BenchmarkManagerDialog.qml` binds the editor pane's minimum to `Math.max(360, editorTable.minimumTableWidth)` (360 still covers the editor toolbar).
`BenchmarkTableModel::identityTexts()` (Q_INVOKABLE, `{categoryId, category, subcategoryId, subcategory, scenario}` per row in row order) feeds the measurement for rows without a delegate, mirroring `tierHeaders()`. `identityContentRevision` re-evaluates it on model reset and on `dataChanged` (renames).
The dialog's own `minimumWidth` stays 900; a library pane at its toolbar width plus a table whose minimum exceeds about 570 px (a very long rank name) can still exceed it.
Tests: `BenchmarkTableModelHeaders.IdentityTextsListEveryRowInRowOrder`, `test_identifyingColumnsShrinkToTheirContentToKeepARankInView` (tst_BenchmarkEditorTable), `test_squeezedEditorKeepsARankColumnInView` (tst_BenchmarkManagerDialog).

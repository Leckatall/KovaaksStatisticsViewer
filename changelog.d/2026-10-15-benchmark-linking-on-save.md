type: fixed
area: Benchmarks
user: Scenarios pasted or typed into a benchmark now link to your runs as soon as you save, instead of showing "Linking to your runs…" until the app restarted, and names that differ from your runs only by capitalisation or extra spaces now match.
---
Old bug: `BenchmarkResolutionUseCase::runReconcilePass` skips automatic linking for the benchmark held by the edit lease, and the lease was only released by `BenchmarkManagerViewModel::discard()` — reached solely from the dirty-close prompt's Discard. Saving kept the lease, and closing the Manager window kept the clean draft open and leased, so a saved benchmark stayed `AutoMappable` ("Linking to your runs…") until a restart built a lease-free `App`.
Fix, two halves: `BenchmarkManagerViewModel::save()` links the candidate through the new `IBenchmarkManagerUseCase::withAutomaticMappings` → `IBenchmarkResolutionUseCase::withAutomaticMappings` (same `computeAutoMappings` path as the reconcile pass, lease-agnostic, persists nothing) before writing, so the saved file, the editor baseline and the progress grid are linked at once. `BenchmarkManagerDialog.qml` now closes a clean draft (`vm().discard()`) when the window closes, including after Save/Discard from the close prompt, so later profile changes link it in the background.
Matching: the resolver's name catalogue is keyed by the new `domain::scenarioNameKey` (trims ASCII whitespace and UTF-8 NBSP, ASCII-lowercases); `resolve()` and `computeAutoMappings()` look entries up by the same key. `ScenarioCandidate` gained `name` (the profile's exact name) and an automatic link now writes it to the entry along with the hash. Played names that fold to the same key become one `Ambiguous` candidate list, so case-only collisions are never auto-linked. The duplicated catalogue construction in `resolve()`/`runReconcilePass()` became one `buildCatalogue()`.

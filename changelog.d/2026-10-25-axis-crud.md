Settings → Graph Lines is split into Lines and Axes tabs. App layer: `renameAxis` in `run_series_editing` beside `createAxis`/`deleteAxis`, and `runAxisLabelAfterDeletingAxis`, which hands the Y-axis choice of a deleted axis to its first enabled line (else its first line, else clears it). `SettingsViewModel` gains `axisOverview`, `renameAxis` and `deleteAxis`, and `createAxis` is now validated; every `createAxis` result carries an `issue` key.

The axis-name rule (trimmed, nonblank, case-insensitively unique) lives in presentation, not in `validateRunSeriesDocument`, so configurations that already persist blank or duplicate names still load. A rejected rename is held as `graphLines` raw input at `axis:<id>` / `name` with `axisNameBlank` or `axisNameDuplicate`, which blocks Save and is cleared by revert. Pending names are re-checked after every axis rename, delete or create, and applied once they pass.

`runAxisLabelChoices` and `yAxisColumnComboBox` are removed: the Y-axis choice is now a per-row "Show on Y-axis" action in the Axes tab. New `AxesPanel.qml`, `AxisNameDialog.qml` (shared by the Axes tab and the line dropdown's "+ New axis…") and `axisNames.js`. The Axes tab's buttons and both axis dialogs' buttons are the new `qml/common/ActionButton.qml`, a `Button` that also activates on Enter, on release like Space.

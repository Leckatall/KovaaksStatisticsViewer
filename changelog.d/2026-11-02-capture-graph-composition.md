`layoutGraphCapture` measures a graph snapshot into a capture item list with wrapped context, axis titles and a passive legend listing each distinct scale once, on one line with its sample when it has a single series. `graphReferenceSize` gives the stable reference size, enlarged only for long content or tier labels, and `suggestGraphResolution` names a lower resolution only after validating it.

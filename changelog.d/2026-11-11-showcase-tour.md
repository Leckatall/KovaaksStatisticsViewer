`showcase` driver tour and its fixture: `tools/driver/specs/showcase/profile.json` plays all 36 Viscose Intermediate S1 scenarios over three months from five representative scenario-specs (new: Polarized Hell, Pasu Angelic, devTS), rescaled to each scenario's own score units, with rows spread across the tiers. VT Controlsphere carries per-second data; its last ten runs use the new `fading-finish` performance-spec, so the Last 10 line falls away from Top 10% late in the run. The tour shoots the Scenarios page, the run graph alone and hovered, the benchmark rows and the rank history in a visible 2x window.

`showcase` driver tour and its fixture: `tools/driver/specs/showcase/profile.json` plays all 36 Viscose Intermediate S1 scenarios over three months from five representative scenario-specs (new: Polarized Hell, Pasu Angelic, devTS), rescaled to each scenario's own score units, with rows spread across the tiers. VT Controlsphere carries per-second data; its last ten runs use the new `fading-finish` performance-spec, so the Last 10 line falls away from Top 10% late in the run. The tour runs headless at 1440x900 and shoots the Scenarios page, the run graph with only those two lines (plain and hovered), the benchmark grid at Control Tracking and at Flick Tech, the rank history and playtime cards, and the Settings dialog's run-graph page.

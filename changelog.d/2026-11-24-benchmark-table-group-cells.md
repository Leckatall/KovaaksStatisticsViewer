`markerSpace` (the slot in front of every scenario name that keeps names aligned beside a row's blocker diamond) is now reserved only while some row blocks, in `BenchmarkProgressGrid.qml` and as `TableMetrics::markerSpace` in the capture. Rows inside groups never carry the diamond, so most benchmarks previously kept an empty 16 px slot in the Scenario column that the group columns could use.
`BenchmarkProgressGrid.qml`: a group column is comfortable with its name and chip side by side (`groupChipUnitWidth` includes the blocker marker). `_measured` adds `categoryMinimum`, `subcategoryMinimum` (each the widest of the header, the longest word and the chip) and `groupsMinimum`, so a spanning category cell keeps its longest word.
`_layoutColumns` gives way in this order: chunk padding first, then Category and Subcategory in proportion to their slack above their minimums, then scenario names elide, then the grid scrolls.
`groupLayouts` keeps the chip beside a one-line name when both fit in the cell. Otherwise the chip gets a line of its own under the name, which wraps greedily in JS with the bold font's whole-line advance. `rowTops` grows a group's last row when its content needs more than its rows give; rows only ever grow. Rows and group cells are positioned from `rowTops` instead of `index * rowHeight`, and `ProgressGridRow`'s value cells centre in the row's real height.
`ProgressGridGroupCell.qml` lays the name and chip out in a `Grid` (two columns when beside) and shows the measured lines verbatim (plain text, fixed line height), so the drawn line breaks match the measured ones. Its content is centred in the group's first row when it fits, and top-padded otherwise.
`table_capture_layout.cpp` follows the same rule: `chipBesideName` now also accounts for the blocker marker, a chip below the name sits `kGroupLineGap` under it, and the name and chip centre on a shared line.
